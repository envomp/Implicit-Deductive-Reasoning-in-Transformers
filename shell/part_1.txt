# Curriculum learning introduces complex training data sequentially. We compared random sampling (random curriculum)
# against deterministic, repeated sampling (eager curriculum). When utilizing the r2 heuristic, the standard “no curriculum” approach
# outperformed curriculum-based methods.

# We also distinguish between random-cot, which samples the next step stochastically from all valid deductions, and
# eager-cot, which deterministically selects the deduction from the first applicable rule in a fixed left-to-right traversal.
# Eager selection combined with the r2 heuristic and no curriculum yielded the highest accuracy.
